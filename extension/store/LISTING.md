# Store listing copy

## Name

ResumeTailor

## Short description (≤132 characters)

Send the job posting you're viewing to your local ResumeTailor app and tailor your resume to it in one click.

## Category

Productivity (Chrome Web Store) · Productivity (Edge Add-ons)

## Detailed description

ResumeTailor tailors your own Word resume to a job description without changing its
formatting. This extension connects your browser to the ResumeTailor app running on
your computer.

- **Capture any job page.** Click the toolbar button, press Alt+Shift+S or right-click
  and choose "Send this page to ResumeTailor". Works on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby,
  Workday, Handshake, LinkedIn, Indeed and most careers pages.
- **Save jobs from search results.** On LinkedIn or Indeed search pages, tick the jobs
  you want and save them to your queue. Each one is completed automatically when you
  open it, so you never copy and paste a description.
- **See what you already track.** A small chip on LinkedIn and Indeed job pages shows
  "In queue" with the job's status, and links to it in the app.
- **Tailor from the page.** Start tailoring (and filling the application form, for
  supported sites) from the popup. Nothing is ever submitted for you.
- **Private by design.** Everything goes to the app on 127.0.0.1. There is no account,
  no cloud service and no tracking.

Requires the free ResumeTailor app: https://github.com/jaydentrannnn/resumetailor
